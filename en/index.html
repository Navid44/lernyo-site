Questions with worked solutions

Real exam questions with step-by-step solutions and hints that show you how to get there yourself.

Lessons

Mock exams

How it works

You tell Lernyo what is coming up – and we plan the rest together.

  1. 1

    Choose your exam

    Pick your federal state, your exam and your subjects – Lernyo knows the exam dates.

  2. 2

  3. 3

    Practise and get help

For parents and teachers

Lernyo works for the whole team

Parents keep an overview, and teachers get the tools to teach online.

For parents

Parents can see exam dates, progress and booked lessons – but only if their child agrees. They can pay for lessons and learning packages, and view or withdraw consents at any time.

For teachers

Teachers offer one-to-one lessons and courses and can bring their own students. An online classroom with whiteboard, calendar and bookings, invoices and payouts are built in – plus a badge for verified teachers.

About us

Made in Frankfurt am Main

Navid Noorgostar founded Lernyo in Frankfurt am Main, Germany, in September 2026.

The idea: everything you need for your exam belongs in one place – real exam questions with worked solutions, short lessons, a study plan up to exam day and teachers for when you get stuck. Matched to your federal state and your exam, without pressure and without advertising profiles.
